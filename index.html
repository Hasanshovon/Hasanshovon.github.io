Research Assistant at Tampere University

Hello! I’m Tanvir, a Research Assistant at FAST-Lab, Tampere University, where I work with Prof. José Martínez Lastra on transformer models for robotic manipulation. I completed my M.Sc. in Signal Processing and Machine Learning at Tampere University, and my B.Sc. in Electronics and Communication Engineering at KUET, Bangladesh.

My research focuses on efficient inference for large models, post-training quantization, interpretability, and multimodal perception for robotics. I am interested in why the cost of a model is distributed so unevenly across its layers — and in what that unevenness lets us take away without losing accuracy.

I am currently applying for fully funded PhD positions starting Fall 2027. Feel free to reach out.

Research

Sensitivity-aware quantization budget allocation

LLaMA-2/3 · Mistral · OPT

Post-training quantization usually applies one recipe uniformly across a network, but weight sensitivity varies sharply with layer depth and role. This work reallocates base precision, group size and outlier rate per layer under a fixed average bit-width — the same budget, spent where it buys accuracy.

Attention efficiency study

GQA · MQA · MLA · TPA · Qwen / Llama

A systematic comparison of attention variants benchmarked together with post-training quantization across small open models, in one harness where the accuracy–latency–memory trade-off is actually comparable.

Multimodal visuo-tactile slip detection

ViViT + TimeSformer · ABB manipulator · RealSense D405

A hybrid dual-backbone architecture fusing RGB and tactile streams to classify slip during grasping, driving graded grip corrections in a closed loop at 13–15 ms per window. M.Sc. thesis work, now under journal review.

Diffusion-based real image editing

Null-text inversion with Delta Denoising Score guidance for text-driven editing of real images, studying how inversion fidelity constrains edit quality.

WinterNav-RUGD

Safety-oriented traversability mapping for small autonomous ground vehicles in winter conditions.
